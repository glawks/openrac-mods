# Revolverator 0.4.0

- Hold Circle facing the Blargian Snagglebeast on Umbris to discover a special
  encounter. The boss lifts briefly before crashing down on Ratchet. A
  three-quarter camera holds through the fade, with a 0.95-second impact shake
  and native dust at the landing point. The game handles Ratchet's death and
  checkpoint return.
- Lift and throw ordinary crates. A strike only grabs one crate from a stack,
  and explosive crates retain their normal detonation. The Nanotech orb and its
  trail stay together while the crate spins or flies. Crates keep Ratchet at full
  movement speed, and jumping is blocked while spinning a crate or enemy.
- Golden impacts use green particles and glow. Blast radius and damage scale
  with charge. The rear valve emits light smoke at idle and more under load.

On clients that expose the official camera API, the Umbris encounter takes
control of the camera only while the scene and death fade need it. The mod
requires interface 1.12 or newer.
